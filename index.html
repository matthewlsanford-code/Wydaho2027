Getting there

Flights: San Diego to Redmond

Everyone flies up Thursday evening, Feb 4 on the Alaska nonstop. Most of us fly home Monday evening, Feb 8; the early families fly home Sunday evening, Feb 7. Redmond (RDM) is the closest airport to Sunriver and Mt. Bachelor.

How we're booking

Each family books its own reservation on the agreed flight numbers. Booking individually keeps Companion Fares, points tickets and Saver fares on the table, which a group booking would rule out.

  • Pay with an Alaska / Atmos Visa so the first checked bag is free for up to 7 people on the reservation.
  • There's no group seat hold, and the 24 of us are about a third of a 76-seat plane. Book soon after we lock the flight numbers.
  • The plane is 2-and-2 seating, so a family of 4 sits in two rows either way.

Fares, cards, points and bags

Cash fares

Recent nonstop round trips have shown up from around $282 in off-peak weeks, with a typical round trip closer to $450. The calculator lets each family pick Saver or Main and enter what they actually see. There are no child fares on domestic flights.

Companion Fare

Atmos Rewards Ascent cardholders can bring a companion for $99 plus taxes from $23 when the cardholder buys a paid fare on the same itinerary. It requires a Main fare, not Saver. At a $450 Main fare it saves about $328.

Flying on points

SAN to RDM is about 830 miles, which sits in Atmos Rewards' 701 to 1,400 mile band. Economy starts at 7,500 points each way, but Alaska prices its own flights by demand, so a February weekend may run higher. Taxes are about $5.60 each way. The calculator uses 12,500 points each way; check the real number on alaskaair.com.

Checked bags and ski gear

Bags run $45 first and $55 second, each way (confirm). With an Alaska / Atmos card paying for the ticket, the first bag is free for up to 7 people on the reservation. Second bags are still charged. A ski or board bag plus one boot bag counts as one checked bag, with no oversize fee up to 115 linear inches and 50 lb.

Rental cars

Built into our pricing

Kids ski free

$0

Offered last season. We're assuming it returns; confirm once 2026/27 deals post.

  • One free ticket for a kid 12 or under for each adult buying a 3+ day lift ticket, for the same days.
  • Must be bought at least 7 days ahead.
  • An adult skiing only 1 or 2 days can't cover a kid, and neither can a parent on the 5-Day Pass.
  • Early families: skiing Sunday makes it a 3-day ticket, which can be cheaper than skipping Sunday once the kids' tickets drop to $0.
Rarely wins for us

5-Day Pass

$449

Launched at $419 in the spring; about $449 since. Confirm today's price.

  • Any 5 days of the 2026/27 season, no blackout dates.
  • Can beat 3 or 4 day tickets for an adult with no kid to cover.
  • It doesn't qualify a kid for Kids Ski Free, so parents covering a kid should buy the multi-day ticket. The calculator picks the cheaper mix.
Group rates: Mt. Bachelor has a groups department, but it doesn't publish group lift prices, and ski clubs that go there mostly book through tour operators. With 24 of us it's worth one email to ask for a group quote and whether it stacks with Kids Ski Free. Kids Ski Free alone likely beats a typical group discount for our mix of 12 adults and 11 skiing kids.
Already have Ikon? Mt. Bachelor is on the Ikon Pass, so anyone holding one is covered. Season passes (Full Season $1,349, Midweek $949) don't pay off for this trip.

Rentals

On-mountain rental packages

  • Packages include skis or board, boots, helmet and poles.
  • Junior packages cover ages 3–12; adult packages 13 and up, in Standard or Performance.
  • Multi-day rentals (2 to 6 days) cost less per day; adult multi-day rentals include free overnight storage at the mountain.
  • Book at least 48 hours ahead for the best price.
